# Homestead Finance

Farm finance and records for homesteads, inside Home Assistant.

> **Early test version.** This app is a work in progress. Don't use it for real records yet.

Track what your animals, crops and farm products cost and earn, and get a clean year-end
pack for your accountant (US Schedule F, cash method). Simple screens on your phone or
computer, opened from the Home Assistant sidebar.

See the **Documentation** tab for details.
